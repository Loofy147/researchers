# Agent Instructions for Mathematical Unification Suite

## Coding Conventions
- Use the 15 predefined mathematical axes in `src/math_unification/data.py` for all framework and phenomena profiling.
- Maintain the ACT-P adversarial protocol versioning (currently v5.3) in the final reports.
- Prioritize **Groundedness**: New phenomena must be mapped to existing axes based on their dominant characteristics.

## Pipeline Flow
1. `src.math_unification.analysis.framework`: Hierarchical clustering and meta-axis profiling.
2. `src.math_unification.analysis.qscore`: Q-score metrics, anchoring, and sensitivity audits.
3. `src.math_unification.synthesis.report`: Final report generation.

## Performance Metrics
- **Morphism Rigor**: Alignment on formal axes (algebra, logic).
- **Conceptual Friction**: Divergence in macro-axis profiles.
- **Gestalt Consistency**: Preservation of framework-specific nuance in synergies.
